Frequently Asked Questions

Are Foamy Soaps good for sensitive skin?

Yes! Our Butter Silk line and Butter Soaps are specifically formulated with nourishing almond oil and oatmeal to calm and protect irritated or sensitive skin types.

What makes the Heavy Duty soap different?

Our new Heavy Duty soap features an advanced natural exfoliator formula designed to deeply clean and scrub away dirt while keeping skin healthy.

Where are your soaps made?

All of our soaps are proudly handcrafted in Canada in small batches to ensure high quality and freshness.

How long does shipping take?

Orders are processed and shipped within 2–3 business days. Standard delivery across Canada typically takes 3–7 business days depending on your location.

How should I store my natural soap bars?

To make your handcrafted soap last as long as possible, keep it on a well-draining soap dish away from direct streams of running water between uses.

Can I pick up my order locally?

Yes! Local pickup options are available during checkout. Select the pickup preference, and we will email you when your order is ready.
